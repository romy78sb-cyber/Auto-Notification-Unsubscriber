Privacy Policy for Auto Notification Unsubscriber
Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Auto Notification Unsubscriber ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our Chrome extension.

1. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, store, use, or share any personal information or browsing data.

No Data Collection: The Extension operates entirely locally within your web browser. It does not collect your personal details, browsing history, IP addresses, or input data.

No External Servers: The Extension does not transmit any data to external servers, third-party services, or databases.

2. How the Extension Works
To perform its core function—helping you manage and block website push notifications—the Extension interacts locally with your browser's native capabilities:

Host Permissions & Content Scripts: The Extension reads the notification permissions of the active webpage solely to determine if a notification control widget should be displayed to you.

Content Settings & Push APIs: When you interact with the extension's control widget to block or unsubscribe from notifications, the command is executed locally using Chrome’s built-in APIs (chrome.contentSettings and navigator.serviceWorker).

3. Third-Party Services
Because the Extension does not collect any data, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third parties.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Extension after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via your developer support contact.
